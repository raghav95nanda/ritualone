# Ritual One nature recordings

These clips were trimmed, softened with frequency filters, normalized to a quiet listening level, faded at the loop boundaries, and encoded to mono MP3.

- Rain: “Rain (1)” by ezwa, released into the public domain. Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_(1).ogg
- Forest: “20090610 0 ambience” by nille, released into the public domain. Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20090610_0_ambience.ogg

Both originals were shared through PDSounds and archived on Wikimedia Commons. Public-domain status was checked on their file description pages on 11 October 2026.
